***Hello World!***

Since I've been doing more and more development stuff lately, I wanted a place where I could explain and write down the things I work on. Sort of like a notebook for the things I've learned, and maybe even something that can serve as a reference or guide for people trying to build the same kinds of things I've been working on.

A lot of the time, the most interesting part of development isn't just getting something to work, but figuring out why it works (and what went wrong along the way). So this is a vault where to keep some of that process, rather than letting all those little lessons disappear into the void.

Here, I’ll be sharing devlogs, notes about gameplay systems, and the occasional deep dive into algorithms. Some posts might be more on the technical side, while others will probably be more about the ideas and problems i find on what I'm building.

Anyway, thanks for dropping by — you’re welcome to stay. Hope you find something worth sticking around for!
